# $20 US Dollar Bills

Place all $20 bill variants in this folder.

## Recommended Images:
- `main.jpg` - Best quality $20 bill (primary reference)
- `variant1.jpg` - Different lighting condition
- `variant2.jpg` - Slightly worn condition
- `series_2013.jpg` - 2013 series design
- `series_2004.jpg` - 2004 series design (if available)

## Image Requirements:
- **Resolution:** 1200x600+ pixels
- **Format:** JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP
- **Quality:** Sharp, clear, good lighting
- **Orientation:** Horizontal preferred

## Expected Accuracy:
- 1 image: 60-70%
- 2-3 images: 80-85%
- 4-5 images: 85-92%
- 6+ images: 90-95%

Add your $20 bill images here and restart the server to load them.
